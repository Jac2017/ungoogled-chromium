# Aether for Chromium: Vision & Design Intent

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** April 2035
**Audience:** Chromium engineers, patch authors, AI developer agents, design reviewers, and anyone building the future of the web browser

---

## The Vision

In 2035, the web browser is no longer a window framed by chrome. It is an **ambient, intent-driven portal** -- a seamless extension of your mind and the physical world around you.

**Aether** is Chromium's evolution of Apple's Liquid Glass design system into a fully volumetric, living interface. Persistent tabs, toolbars, omniboxes, and buttons disappear. In their place is a subtle **volumetric field of light and presence** -- intelligent, refractive, biomorphic ether that manifests exactly when and where your intent requires it, then dissolves instantly.

Aether does not fight for your attention. It amplifies it. It turns Chromium (and every fork -- Chrome, Edge, Brave, and beyond) into a calm, magical partner that feels like the air you breathe digitally.

---

## Core Intent of Aether

### Intent-driven & zero-UI by default

No more always-visible chrome. The interface is invisible until your gaze, subtle gesture, voice, or neural cue summons it. Information and controls appear as gentle, glowing ether fields that respect your focus and the real-world lighting in your room.

### Liquid Light evolved into volumetric presence

Liquid Glass taught us that UI can feel alive, translucent, and harmonious on flat screens. Aether takes that foundation and lifts it into true 3D space: refractive glows, soft caustics, breathing physics, and subtle parallax that interact with your content, your environment, and your biology.

### Content-first, harmonious, and deeply personal

The web page itself remains the hero. Aether layers float above it like intelligent light, never competing. The system is hyper-personalized by on-device AI, adapting to your workflow, mood, lighting conditions, and even micro-expressions -- all while preserving Chromium's core promise of an open, extensible, cross-platform web.

### Multimodal and future-proof

Today it works beautifully with gaze tracking, air gestures, voice, and trackpad/keyboard. Tomorrow it will natively support neural input. Aether is designed to scale from today's laptops and desktops into tomorrow's spatial and AR experiences without breaking compatibility.

## What Using Aether in Chromium Feels Like (2035 Everyday Scenarios)

You open Chromium on your Mac, Windows, or Linux machine. The screen is almost entirely your content -- clean, spacious, serene.

### Morning news scan

You glance toward the top edge of the screen. A faint golden ether field materializes in your peripheral vision -- your personalized constellation of 8 live tab previews (Fox News, YouTube, Wikipedia, Amazon AirPods product page, Delta flight search, Gmail, Nordstrom Frame Jeans, Apple.com) orbiting gently in a soft arc. No tab bar. No clutter. Just living previews connected by delicate refractive strands. You dwell your gaze on one; it blooms forward with a liquid-light animation and expands into full view.

### Deep research or shopping

While reading a long article, related links appear as faint ether strands that pulse only when your eyes linger. Need to compare products? Say "compare" or think it -- a volumetric comparison layer unfolds in mid-air above the page, with 3D models and specs that you can rotate naturally with gestures or trackpad.

### Switching contexts

Voice command "open Gmail" or a quick neural cue, and the current page dissolves with elegant refractive trails while Gmail materializes seamlessly. The old tab preview gently orbits back into the constellation.

### Focus mode

When you're in flow, Aether fades even further -- leaving only the purest content and the faintest breathing glow at the edges. It actively protects your attention by dimming distractions and suggesting breaks only when your biometrics indicate fatigue.

### Power-user moments

For developers or heavy tab users, you can summon the full Aether controls -- a customizable, semi-transparent volumetric dashboard that still feels like evolved Liquid Glass but now with depth, generative layouts, and AI-suggested reorganizations.

## Why Chromium Needs Aether

Chromium powers the majority of the world's web browsers. It has always been about openness, performance, and extensibility. Aether honors that legacy by:

- **Building directly on today's Chromium capabilities** (WebGPU, OffscreenCanvas, experimental input APIs, Blink compositor) so the vision is shippable today behind flags.
- **Preserving full web compatibility** -- no breakage of extensions, sites, or existing workflows.
- **Giving every Chromium-based browser a path** to the same delightful, human-centered future that Apple is pioneering with Safari and visionOS.

Aether is not a skin or theme. It is a fundamental shift in how we relate to the browser: from managing windows and chrome to simply thinking with the web.

**The interface becomes the breath of intelligence** -- elegant, calm, invisible until needed, and profoundly helpful.
